Ride Speed

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Introduction

Ride Speed shows your live speed in a floating overlay while you use navigation and ride-hailing apps. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Ride Speed does not operate its own servers and does not require you to create an account.

Information the app accesses

Location

Ride Speed uses your device's location to calculate and display your current speed. Location is processed on your device to show the overlay and is not sent to any server operated by Ride Speed.

App Usage Access

With your permission, Ride Speed uses App Usage Access to detect when a supported navigation or ride-hailing app is in the foreground, so the overlay can be shown at the right time. This information is used on your device only.

Display over other apps

Ride Speed uses the "Display over other apps" permission to show the floating speed overlay on top of other apps. This permission does not give the app access to the content of those apps.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time in your device's Android settings. Some features may stop working if a permission is revoked.

Third-party services

Ride Speed uses the following third-party services, which may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies:

Google AdMob

Used to display ads in the app. AdMob may collect device information such as the advertising ID to serve and measure ads. Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information in ads

Firebase Analytics

Used to understand how the app is used so it can be improved. It collects usage and device information. Firebase Privacy and Security

Firebase Crashlytics

Used to collect crash reports so problems in the app can be identified and fixed. Crash reports may include device and app state information at the time of the crash. Firebase Privacy and Security

Data sharing and retention

Ride Speed does not sell your personal information. Apart from the third-party services listed above, Ride Speed does not share your information with others.

Because Ride Speed does not operate its own servers, it does not store your information on its own systems. Information collected by the third-party services above is retained according to their respective policies.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Ride Speed removes the app and its locally stored data from your device. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.

If you have questions about your data, contact us at ridespeed07@gmail.com.

Safety notice

Do not interact with the app while driving or riding. Always follow traffic laws and pay attention to the road. The speed shown is an estimate based on your device's location and may not be accurate.

Children

Ride Speed is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
